Sonic 3: experimental local test

This is not full Sonic 3 support. Walking, jumping, rolling, spindash, plane switches, moving platforms, directional springs, timed spikes, collapsing platforms, item boxes, shields, speed shoes, rings, dropped-ring recovery, and checkpoints are implemented. Tails can fly. The stage's water level changes movement, with 30 seconds of air unless protected by a bubble shield. Cyclone, Saruder, and Tulippon have experimental patrols and projectile attacks, using the original sprites. Sonic can use elemental shield moves. Swing ropes can be grabbed by either airborne player; press jump to release with the rope's momentum. Eight rock piles now have solid collision and shared destruction using their original sprites. Jump onto top-breakable piles, or roll into side-breakable piles at speed; Sonic's fire dash can also break sides. In this experiment, piles with neither smash flag accept either attack. Character-restricted piles stay solid. One custom rock variant and the tile-backed breakable barriers remain unsupported. The fling ramp launches either grounded player approaching in its allowed direction at four pixels per tick or faster. Lives are counted, but this test always respawns players rather than ending the game. Tile surface grip now supports walls and ceilings with sufficient speed, but is still experimental physics. No bosses or stage progression yet; several stage objects remain unsupported. Referenced music and sound files are missing from this archive. Online testing is opt-in, with local input prediction and buffered rendering between server snapshots.

Player 1: arrows + Z to jump. Player 2: WASD + J to jump. Down while moving rolls. Hold down and tap jump to charge a spindash; release down to launch. Tails: release and press jump again in midair to fly, then hold jump to climb. Flight lasts eight seconds. Sonic: press jump again in midair with a lightning shield to double jump, a fire shield to dash, or a bubble shield to bounce. Gamepads 1 and 2 are separate.
